Courier Field – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Courier Field (package com.pokoparcel.courierfield), published on Google Play by Mahmoud Abdelhakeem ("we"), handles information. Courier Field is made for players of all ages, including children.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no in-app purchases and no way to enter names, photos, contact details or location.

Your game progress (coins, unlocked vans and roof toppers, stars, quests and the sound setting) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

Courier Field shows ads from Google AdMob: optional "Watch ad" videos that give an in-game reward, and an occasional full-screen ad between shifts. Because the game is played by children, every ad request is set up to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws:

  • Requests are tagged as child-directed and under the age of consent, so Google serves only non-personalised ads and does not use interest-based advertising or remarketing.
  • Ad content is limited to the G (general audiences) rating.
  • The game does not request the Android Advertising ID permission or the Android Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software may process limited technical information such as your device's IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating system type, and ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped). This processing is done by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and child-directed treatment

Watching ads is never required to play. Rewarded ads only start when the player taps a button clearly labelled "Watch ad".

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Courier Field takes part in Google Play's Families programme and uses only advertising that is certified for that programme and configured as described above. If you believe a child's personal information has been collected through the game, please contact us and we will work with Google to address it.

Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal information, we hold none to secure or delete. Ad requests made by Google's software are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Your choices

  • You can play without ever choosing to watch a rewarded ad.
  • You can delete all saved game data at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
